Most on-page SEO checklists are bloated with things that don't move rankings. Here's the shorter list I actually run through on client sites.

## 1. One clear target keyword per page

If a page is trying to rank for five things, it usually ranks for none of them well. Pick one primary keyword and two or three close variants.

## 2. Title tag says what the page is about — in the first 60 characters

Search engines truncate long titles. Front-load the important words.

## 3. The H1 isn't the same as the title tag

Give people a reason to keep reading once they've clicked, not a repeat of what they already saw in search results.
